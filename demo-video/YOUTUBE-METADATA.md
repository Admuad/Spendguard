## Description (paste into the Description box)

SpendGuard is a scoped session-key vault for tUSD8 on the Aevum testnet. Instead of handing your AI agent your private key, you grant it a session key that can only pay approved recipients, within a cap, before an expiry. Take it back in one click.

In this demo, I deposit 3.00 tUSD8, grant a key with a 1.00 cap, pay 0.10 to a merchant, revoke the key, and sweep the vault back to the owner. Everything happens on chain, on the Aevum testnet.
