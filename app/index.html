Deposit tUSD8

Only the vault owner can deposit from this page. Connect the owner wallet to fund this vault.

Connect a browser wallet (MetaMask, Rabby, or any injected wallet) to deposit. The vault data above is live and readable without a wallet.

Pay as a session key

A granted key pays merchants within its bounds. Only the key itself can call. No one can spend another key's session.

Connect a browser wallet (MetaMask, Rabby, or any injected wallet) to pay as a session key. The vault data above is live and readable without a wallet.

Owner controls

Pause freezes payments instantly; the sweep always works, paused or not. Recovery is never locked behind a pause.

Connect a browser wallet (MetaMask, Rabby, or any injected wallet) to use owner controls. The vault data above is live and readable without a wallet.

Grant a session key

The owner grants a key its bounds: who it may pay, how much per payment, how much in total, and until when.

Connect a browser wallet (MetaMask, Rabby, or any injected wallet) to grant a session. The vault data above is live and readable without a wallet.

Sessions

Every session key ever granted to this vault, with its live on-chain state. Revoking is immediate and irreversible. A revoked key can never spend again. Bounds can be edited while a session is active; an edit never resets what the key has spent.
